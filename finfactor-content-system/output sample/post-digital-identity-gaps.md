## POST COPY

15 to 30% of OTP attempts fail in Tier 2 and Tier 3 markets (Rohit Taneja, Moneycontrol, May 2026).

Credit teams at NBFCs expanding into lower-tier geographies have absorbed this as operational reality, a known cost of working where signal is thin. Taneja's argument is that it's a fixable system gap. The OVSE framework verifies Aadhaar through a cryptographically signed QR code: no OTP, no mobile number dependency, completing in under 60 seconds anywhere.

Pull that bottleneck out and KYC abandonment drops 60–80%, onboarding runs 3–5x faster than branch-based processes, identity fraud at origination falls 30–50% (Taneja, Moneycontrol).

The underlying structure — consent-first, audit-logged, DPDP 2023-aligned — is where the consented data stack is heading anyway.

Credit and digital heads building lower-tier funnels now are making an infrastructure decision, not just an operations one. Worth reading.
